FEMS stations across the Southwest

Every RAWS station in the Southwest PSAs, with that day's FEMS values. A dot's color shows how unusual the value is for that station's own history. PSAs are filled by the workbook ERC(Y) or BI percentile class. Click anything for details, and zoom with the mouse wheel or +/−.

Gray dots have no report that day. Dark-ringed dots are the workbook's key stations, the ones used for the PSA ERC(Y)/BI averages.

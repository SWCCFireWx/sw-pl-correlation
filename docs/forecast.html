Southwest PL – 7-day outlook

The chance the Southwest PL is 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 on each of the next 7 days, learned from the archive. It uses only observed FEMS values, fire starts and the PL history, with no weather forecasts. The Southwest row uses every PSA together. Each PSA row uses only that PSA's own data, so you can see which parts of the Southwest are pushing toward a higher PL. This is not the 7-Day Significant Fire Potential product.

How well has this worked?

Each past year was predicted by a model trained on the other years, then compared with the simplest forecast: "PL stays where it is." Because the PL rarely changes, "stays the same" is right most days. The columns to watch are the PL changes the model caught, and how often it was right when it called a change. Refreshed once a day.

What goes in

  • Southwest: today's PL; the average PSA ERC(Y) and BI percentile, using your breakpoints workbook; how many PSAs are at or above the 90th and 97th; Southwest-median ERC, BI, 1000-hr fuel moisture and KBDI; 7-day changes; new fire starts over the last 7 days; time of year (pre-monsoon or monsoon).
  • Each PSA: the same, using only that PSA. Its key-station ERC(Y) and BI averages and percentiles come from the workbook; the other values are the median of all RAWS in the PSA. Fire starts are only those that began in the PSA.
  • Lightning isn't used, because NOAA posts it weeks late. No weather forecasts are used.
